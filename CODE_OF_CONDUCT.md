# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We pledge to make participation in MARIA Flight Radar a harassment-free
experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity,
gender identity and expression, level of experience, nationality, personal
appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our Standards

Examples of positive behavior include being respectful, welcoming different
viewpoints, accepting constructive feedback, and focusing on what is best for
the community. Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, intimidation,
discrimination, publishing others' private information, and other conduct that
could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement Responsibilities

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying standards and taking fair,
appropriate corrective action in response to unacceptable behavior.

## Scope

This Code applies within project spaces and when an individual officially
represents the project in public spaces.

## Enforcement

Report incidents to project maintainers through GitHub Security Advisories or
repository reporting channels. Reports will be reviewed and handled as
confidentially as practical.

Maintainers may issue a correction, warning, temporary ban, or permanent ban
depending on impact and pattern of behavior.
